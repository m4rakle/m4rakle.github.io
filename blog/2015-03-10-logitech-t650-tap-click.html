Enable tap-click for Logitech T650

Legacy post from Notes, Hacks, Thoughts. Kept as a historical note; hardware, firmware, and software details may be outdated.

Tap-click is not enabled out of the box for the Logitech T650. Logitech did not provide Linux support. Here are the quick steps to enable tap-click.

  1. If your touchpad is connected to the computer with a USB lead, unplug it.
  2. Download the firmware update from Logitech support for the Touchpad T650, then run it in Windows.
  3. Download the Unifying software from the same support page. Choose advanced options when setting it up.
  4. The software should already detect the touchpad. Click "update firmware" and follow the directions.

That is it.
