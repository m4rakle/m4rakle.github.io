Solution: Asus Z97 motherboard stuck at BIOS splash screen

Legacy post from my Blogger.

For some reason, after I tweaked my operating system installations for some time, the machine would get stuck at the splash screen while booting. I could not even get into the BIOS settings.

My fix: unplug the hard disk drive. For me it was an SSD. Start the computer; now I can get into the BIOS settings. Then I plug the hard disk drive in again.

I tried unplugging other devices, but had no luck. I guess the BIOS was buggy and unable to load one of the attached devices.

There were some other weird things with this motherboard too. Every time I tried to enable VT-x in BIOS, it automatically shut down, the motherboard light flashed a few times, reset BIOS, and started up with "power surge detected." I had not found a solution yet.
